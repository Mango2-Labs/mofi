# Mofi

Invest in stocks, ETFs, pre-IPO companies and crypto 24/7, from $1, with dollars from any chain.

## What you can do

- **Sign in with email.** You get a self-custodial Solana wallet (Privy). No seed phrase and no SOL: Mofi pays the network fees.
- **Deposit from any chain.** Permanent deposit addresses on Tron, BNB Chain, Ethereum, Base, Robinhood Chain and more (Aurora Intents). Money arrives as USDC cash.
- **Buy and sell** stocks, ETFs, pre-stocks (SpaceX, OpenAI, Anthropic) and crypto on Jupiter. Short on cash? "Deposit $X and buy" buys as soon as the deposit lands.
- **Withdraw to any chain** (Aurora Intents), with fees shown on top. An optional **Private** mode (Aurora confidential swaps) hides the link between your Mofi wallet and the receiving address.
- **Borrow** USDC against your stocks (Kamino), capped at half of Kamino's limit.
- **Earn** interest on idle cash in Kamino's Steakhouse USDC vault.
- **Activity** lists every deposit, buy, sale, withdrawal and loan, each linked to Solscan.
